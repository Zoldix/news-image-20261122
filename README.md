One image summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: 7 dated stories across 4 regions, led by the COP31 climate deal taking shape at dawn in Antalya, with Hong Kong voting for 723 seats and Nepal's PM landing in Delhi, plus Australia's AUKUS rebuttal and the Las Vegas GP tonight.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Sunday 22 November 2026 (UTC). Anything from 21 November or earlier — the Banda Sea M6.8 quake, the Iran strikes on Bahrain bases, the Sparta II tanker fire, the Peru quake, the "Chapo Isidro" arrest, the Ukraine barrage, the FBI database hack, the EU–Indonesia deal — is named in the DATE CHECK box as deliberately left out, not presented as Sunday's news. Claims carry their label (CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM / FORECAST) and their source.

The image is hand-drawn with Pillow (no photos); `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies it to `/output/entry.png`, deterministically.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
